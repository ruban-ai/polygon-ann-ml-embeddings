## IMPORTANT for the paper framing (found while checking ShapeToVec+'s tables)
ShapeToVec+ Table 1 (Parks / Water / Sports) is computed on **3K-dimensional** vectors. Its "32x" is
relative to 3K-d float32, i.e. BAE-1bit Parks = ~384 bytes/polygon at 76% R@50.
Our "47x" is relative to ShapeToVec's **12K-d** float32; our 256-d fp32 embedding is 1,024 bytes,
i.e. ~2.7x LARGER than BAE-1bit in absolute bytes (but at 92% vs 76% R@50).
=> "exceeds ShapeToVec+'s compression" (abstract, intro, contribution 1) is true only as a ratio against
different baselines, not in bytes per polygon. A reviewer who knows ShapeToVec+ will catch this.
Options to discuss with Buddhi: (a) compare in bytes/polygon explicitly; (b) quantize our embedding
(e.g. 256-d uint8/fp16 = 256/512 B) -- likely near-lossless and would beat BAE-1bit on bytes and recall;
(c) report our 64/128-d widths on Parks too (needs a Parks run with {64..} prefixes).
